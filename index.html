Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Smart Calculator & Converter collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or government-issued identification numbers.

On-Device Calculations: All numerical inputs, financial values (e.g., loan amounts, interest rates, salary figures, investments), property dimensions, and unit conversions you calculate inside the App are processed strictly on your device in real-time. None of your calculations are stored on our servers or transmitted to any third party.

b. Non-Personal & Technical Information

When you access and use the App, certain non-personal device information may be automatically processed by third-party SDKs to provide core app functionality and relevant advertising:

  • Device model, manufacturer, and screen resolution
  • Operating system and version
  • App version and build number
  • Mobile network state, Wi-Fi connectivity status, and carrier name
  • Coarse geographical location derived from IP address (country / city level only)
  • Advertising Identifier (Google Advertising ID)

2. Third-Party Services & In-App Advertising

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to display advertisements, analyze performance, and deliver remote app configurations.

a. Start.io (In-App Advertising)

We use Start.io (formerly StartApp) to deliver in-app advertisements (such as banner and interstitial ads) to keep our App free. Start.io may collect and process information such as your device identifiers, advertising ID, IP address, device specifications, and network status to serve advertisements and measure ad performance.

  • Start.io Privacy Policy: https://www.start.io/policy/privacy-policy/
  • Start.io Data Subject Rights & Opt-Out: https://www.start.io/policy/opt-out/

b. Google Firebase

We use Google Firebase services to maintain app stability, deliver dynamic updates, and understand user navigation:

  • Google & Firebase Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. How We Use the Information

The information processed by third-party services is used exclusively to:

  • Operate, maintain, and optimize app features and performance
  • Notify users about critical app updates and improvements
  • Diagnose technical bugs and prevent crashes
  • Display non-intrusive advertisements to support ongoing free development

4. Data Storage and Retention

We do not operate backend servers that store your personal data or calculator histories. Any local preferences (such as selected language, theme, or recent unit selections) are stored solely in your device's local memory and are erased when you clear app data or uninstall the App.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application is not targeted towards children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.

6. Your Privacy Rights and Opt-Out Choices

You have full control over your privacy and advertising preferences:

  • Reset or Limit Advertising ID on Android: Open your device's Settings > Google > Ads and tap "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID".
  • Start.io Opt-Out: You can exercise your opt-out rights directly through Start.io's portal at https://www.start.io/policy/opt-out/ .

7. Security

We value your trust. We implement standard industry security practices and do not transmit sensitive calculation data over the network. However, please remember that no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our app features, SDKs, or legal requirements. Any modifications will be effective immediately upon posting the updated policy on this page.
